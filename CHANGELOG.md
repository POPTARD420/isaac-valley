# Changelog

## 0.8.0
- Works on Windows as well as Mac (and Linux): finds Isaac's mods folder and your extracted
  files automatically on each system.
- Bombs look exactly like your Isaac bombs, including Hot, Sad, Butt, Bob's Curse, Glitter,
  Bobby and Fast bombs, and their flashing before they explode.
- Transformations: Guppy, Beelzebub, Fun Guy, Seraphim, Bob, Spun, Mom, Conjoined,
  Leviathan, Oh Crap, Bookworm, Adult and Spider Baby.

## 0.7
- Engine mode, the SkyCraft way: Isaac's own engine runs your attacks and Stardew draws them.
- Isaac's charge bar, sound effects, homing at Stardew monsters, bombs that break rocks.

## 0.1 – 0.6
- Play as Isaac with his animations and item costumes, shared stats and hearts,
  tears, items, bombs on E.
